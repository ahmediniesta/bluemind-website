What happens next

  1. You get a reference number right away and an email receipt.
  2. Our intake team calls the parent or guardian within two business days.
  3. We confirm the diagnosis and insurance, and request authorization from the county.
  4. A BCBA schedules the assessment and the plan takes shape.

You receive an email each time the status changes, and you can check the status any time with your reference number.
