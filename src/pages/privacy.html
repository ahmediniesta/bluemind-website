Privacy

Website privacy notice

What we collect on this website, why, and how it is protected.

What this site collects

This website collects information only when you choose to send it to us through one of three forms: the referral form, the job application form and the contact form. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

Referrals

A referral includes health information about a child, such as a diagnosis and insurance details. It is sent directly to our secure intake system, is visible only to BlueMind intake and clinical staff who need it, and is used solely to evaluate and provide services. Health information is handled under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Families receive our full Notice of Privacy Practices at intake.

Job applications

Applications and resumes go to our hiring team and are used only for recruiting. We keep them on file for six months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

Messages

Contact-form messages go to our intake team so we can reply. Please do not include medical details in a message; we will ask for what we need by phone.

Technical details

Forms are sent over an encrypted connection to our intake system. We record the submitting connection's address for abuse prevention only. This website uses no cookies of its own; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's standard request information.
