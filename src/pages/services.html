ABA therapy, built around your child

Every family at BlueMind gets a Board Certified Behavior Analyst, a trained behavior technician, an individualized plan and a say in where and how therapy happens.

What ABA is, in plain terms

Applied Behavior Analysis is the science of how people learn. In practice it means we look closely at what your child can do today, pick the next small step toward a goal, teach it in a way your child finds motivating, and measure whether it worked. Then we do it again.

Goals come from your family's priorities: asking for help instead of melting down, playing with a sibling, getting dressed, tolerating a haircut, making a friend at school. Teaching happens through play, everyday routines and structured practice, always with positive reinforcement.

Where therapy happens

Four settings, one plan

The BCBA recommends settings based on your child's goals. Most families combine center sessions with home or school time, and the plan can shift as needs change.

{{icon:building}}

At our centers

Therapy rooms stocked with the toys, materials and quiet spaces each program needs, peers nearby for social practice, and the clinical team down the hall. Ypsilanti, Dearborn and Fort Gratiot.

{{icon:home}}

At home

The best place to teach routines: mornings, meals, play with siblings, bedtime. Parents see the strategies in action and practice them with the technician.

{{icon:school}}

At school

With the school's agreement, a technician supports your child in class and at recess, and the BCBA coordinates with teachers so everyone works from the same plan.

{{icon:map}}

In the community

Stores, parks, the library, the doctor's office. Skills are practiced in the places they will actually be used, with telehealth available for parent training and supervision when it helps.

What is included

The full picture of care

{{icon:clipboard}}

Initial assessment and treatment plan

A BCBA meets your child and family, completes a skills and behavior assessment (tools such as the VB-MAPP or ABLLS-R), and writes a treatment plan with measurable goals, recommended hours and settings. You review it before therapy begins, and it goes to your county program for authorization.

{{icon:star}}

One-on-one direct therapy

A trained behavior technician works individually with your child, following the programs in the plan, teaching through play and daily activities, and collecting data every session so the BCBA can see what is working.

{{icon:badge}}

BCBA supervision and program updates

The BCBA observes sessions, coaches the technician, reviews the data and updates programs. Supervision is scheduled as a regular share of your child's therapy hours, not an afterthought.

{{icon:family}}

Parent and caregiver training

Regular sessions with your BCBA, in person or by telehealth, to learn the strategies in your child's plan, troubleshoot tough moments at home and plan for transitions like a new school year.

{{icon:chart}}

Progress reports and reassessment

Written progress reports summarize what your child has mastered and what comes next. Every six months the BCBA reassesses, updates goals and requests the next authorization, with no gap in care.

{{icon:chat}}

Coordination with your team

With your permission we coordinate with your child's pediatrician, school, speech and occupational therapists and county supports coordinator, so everyone is pulling in the same direction.

Our approach

Positive, playful, precise

We use positive reinforcement and naturalistic teaching: following your child's interests, building on what they already love, and celebrating every step. We are precise about data because it tells us when to push, when to pause and when a program needs to change.

And we are honest about progress. You will always know what the goals are, how your child is doing and what we are changing next.
