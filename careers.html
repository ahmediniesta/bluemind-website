Do work that matters, every single day

We hire Behavior Technicians and Board Certified Behavior Analysts for all three of our centers. Full-time and part-time schedules, real supervision, and a team that takes care of its people.

Why BlueMind

What you get here

Real supervision

BCBAs who observe your sessions, coach you and are reachable when a session gets hard. Supervision hours that count toward your RBT and BCBA requirements.

Training from day one

Structured onboarding, hands-on training with experienced technicians and ongoing competency checks, so you never feel thrown in.

Room to grow

Technicians become lead technicians, trainers and supervisors. BCBAs grow into clinical director roles as our centers grow.

Three centers, four counties

Work at the center nearest you in Ypsilanti, Dearborn or Fort Gratiot, with in-home and school sessions close to home.

Behavior Technician (RBT)

Deliver one-on-one ABA sessions under BCBA supervision, at a center, in homes and at schools. Run programs, collect data, write session notes and celebrate progress with families.

You bring: a high school diploma or higher, patience and energy for working with children, reliable transportation, and a clean background check. RBT credential preferred; we train and support candidates who are working toward it.

Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA)

Carry a manageable caseload, lead assessments and treatment planning, supervise and mentor technicians, and run parent training. In-person at a center with telehealth for some supervision and family sessions.

You bring: current BCBA certification, Michigan licensure as a behavior analyst (or eligibility), and experience with early intervention or school-age programs.
