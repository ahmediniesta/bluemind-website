About BlueMind

A team that shows up for kids

BlueMind Therapy started in Ypsilanti with a simple idea: children with autism deserve therapy that is individualized, evidence-based and delivered with warmth. Today we serve families from three centers and across four counties.

Our mission

To help children and teens with autism build the communication, social and daily living skills that open up their world, and to give their families the tools and confidence to keep that progress going.

We measure ourselves by what changes at home: the first spontaneous request, the first playdate that goes well, the morning routine that finally runs smoothly.

How we work

What you can count on

Individualized

No two plans are the same. Goals come from your child's assessment and your family's priorities, and they change as your child grows.

Family-centered

Parents are partners. You see the plan, you learn the strategies, and you decide with us where and when therapy happens.

Evidence-based

Every program is grounded in the research base of ABA, measured session by session, and adjusted by a BCBA when the data says so.

Compassionate

Therapy should feel good. We teach through play, lead with reinforcement and treat every child with dignity.

Our team

Who works with your child

Each center runs with the same structure, so families get the same standard of care in Ypsilanti, Dearborn and Fort Gratiot.

Board Certified Behavior Analysts

BCBAs assess, write treatment plans, supervise technicians and lead parent training. Each one carries a caseload small enough to know every child well and holds current certification with the Behavior Analyst Certification Board.

Clinical directors

A BCBA clinical director leads each center: reviewing quality, mentoring the clinical team and making sure every plan meets our standards and the county's requirements.

Behavior technicians

Technicians deliver the one-on-one sessions. They are trained and credentialed as Registered Behavior Technicians, supervised by a BCBA, and chosen for patience, energy and a love of working with kids.

Intake, scheduling and front desk

The people who answer the phone, confirm authorizations with your county, build your child's schedule and greet you at the door. They are part of the care team too.

Safety and compliance

  • Every team member passes criminal background and Michigan child-abuse registry checks before working with children
  • Medicaid-enrolled ABA provider with Washtenaw County CMH, DWIHN and St. Clair County CMH
  • Health information handled under HIPAA, with records kept in secure clinical systems
  • Clinical records reviewed regularly against county quality standards

Diversity, equity and inclusion

Everyone belongs here

BlueMind is committed to a workplace and a clinic where people of every background are welcomed, heard and supported. We hire, train and promote without regard to race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, language, age, disability or socioeconomic status, and we actively recruit a team that reflects the communities we serve.

For families, that means care that respects your language, your culture and your goals, with interpreters arranged whenever they are needed. This is ongoing work, and we hold ourselves accountable for it.
