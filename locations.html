Visiting

What to expect at a center

Our centers are designed for children: therapy rooms with the materials each program needs, open play areas for social practice, quiet spaces for breaks, and a front desk that knows every family by name. Parents are welcome to observe sessions and meet with the BCBA on site.

  • Free parking at every center
  • Interpreter services arranged on request
  • Tours available for families considering BlueMind
Book a tourCall (734) 252-5215

Beyond the centers

Not near one of our buildings? In-home and school-based therapy runs across all four counties we serve, scheduled from the nearest center. Tell us where you live on the referral form and we will match you with the right team.
