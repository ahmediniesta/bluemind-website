# Photos

Drop real photos here and the site shows them automatically. Until a file exists, the page
shows an illustrated panel in its place.

| File | Where it appears | Suggested shot |
|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Home page, top right | A bright therapy room or play area, no people or staff only |
| `ypsilanti.jpg` | Locations page and footer | The Ypsilanti center, outside or lobby |
| `dearborn.jpg` | Locations page | The Dearborn center |
| `fort-gratiot.jpg` | Locations page | The Fort Gratiot center |
| `team.jpg` | About page | Staff who have given written consent |
| `careers.jpg` | Careers page | A training or team moment, staff consent only |

Rules: never a client, a client's first name, artwork, or anything that could identify a
child. Staff photos need the staff member's written consent. Landscape, at least 1600 px
wide, JPEG, under 600 KB each (export at quality 80).
